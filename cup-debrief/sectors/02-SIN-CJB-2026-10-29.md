## Knowledge
- box-setup: TROP — the higher is more conservative
- box-setup: wind modelling — request the lower and highest FL and the FMS will interpolate; insert CLB winds if there is time, also history winds
- cruise: weather deviation — 1-in-60 rule; use your heading to determine the offset: 10 degrees = 10 miles
- cruise: avoiding a super cell — 8000 ft and 60 NM
- descent: TAS increases about 2-3 kts per 1000 ft of elevation; take Vapp + wind factor, GS/2 gives your V/S, that is the estimated ROD
- cruise: EDTO — fuel leak, smoke, dual engine failure, fire = fixed speed strategy VMO/MMO; other cases, even medical, follow the standard strategy — no rush, more stable approach, no need to overstress the aircraft with a chance of overspeed
- pushback: sequence — 1 aerobridge retract; 2 tow truck connect, NW STRG; 3 3-5 mins to remove chocks (8 chocks, 3 grounding cables); 4 before headset connect tell FO to call for clearance when he hears it; 5 headset connect
- descent: reduce to 250 kts is enough; wait till 25 NM to 3200
- approach: direct to the point before the FAF to have a proper turn
- approach: can configure gear down a bit later, otherwise you are dragging in
- parking: flow — 1 start APU; 2 320 identified; 3 lights off / brake fans on / pressure is good; 4 area is clear
