# Scripts (D items)

Fixed scripts and flows, read aloud the night before. Bracketed text is yours to fill
from the manuals; everything else is lifted from the sector notes. `route.py` appends
every new D item under **Logged gaps** so you can see what still needs writing in.

## CC briefing

1. Brief CC **first**, at the gate, then give AWNF (S2)
2. Introductions, who is PF/PM
3. Flight time, cruise level, **taxi minimum 10 mins out of SG, not 5** (S1)
4. **Weather**: en route and destination, turbulence expected, seat-belt plan (S2, S3)
5. **Layer-2 service**: how you can help them — will they be done with service by then; you will release them after (S1, S2, S3). If you hold the seat belts for weather, tell them to hold the service (S2)
6. **O procedure** — take it out and read it, don't skip (S3)
7. Security / sterile cockpit, special loads (NOTOC: go through it with FO, S3), MEL with cabin impact
8. Questions

## Takeoff briefing

1. Runway and intersection — say the right one (02C not 20C, S2); read the chart number (S3)
2. Performance, flex/TOGA, config; idle factor checked (S1)
3. **RTO actions** (S2)
4. **EFATO actions** (S2)
5. **TEM** for *this* takeoff: taxi route traps in the INTAM (T9 when 02L, S2), MEL impact (S3), TCAS threats as "conflicting aircraft" (S3), weather, fatigue — and the mitigation for each
6. Departure routing, first altitude; if a direct is likely, know the general heading (S4)
7. Dual cooling / SETO flow differences (S1, S3)

## Prelims flow (S1)

1. Let FO go in and take the iPad, or get seated first
2. Seat, cockpit lights, headset, paper, licence, flow prep checklist
3. Fuel page + recall
4. Check books
5. Briefing
6. Preliminary fuel if you have it; FO goes down, you fill the fuel cockpit column

## Load sheet sequence (S1, S5)

1. Put in the figures and cross-check
2. Write the pax on my sheet
3. Go through with FO
4. Get the latest weather, then cross-check performance — **redo perf if the figures changed**

## Post box-setup scan (S1)

CLB NAV BLUE 1FD2 · QNH 1013 · PFD 20 ft / ISIS 10 ft · HDG on standby · landing elevation AUTO · status good · navs aligned · hat check, progress page for OPT FL

## Pushback sequence (S2)

1. Aerobridge retract
2. Tow truck connect — NW STRG
3. 3-5 mins to remove chocks (8 chocks, 3 grounding cables)
4. Before headset connect: tell FO to call for clearance when he hears it
5. Headset connect
6. China: beacon on first, then call pushback to get the tow truck connected (S5)

## Parking flow (S1, S2, S4)

1. **APU on before the turn-in**
2. Taxi-in speed — slow before the turn
3. 320 identified; VDGS calibrates from 20 m, nose over the line at 18 m, walking-speed braking
4. Lights off / brake fans on / pressure good / area clear
5. Check doors before releasing brakes
6. Eng off — spool down first, then call doors to disarm
7. SD page back to default
