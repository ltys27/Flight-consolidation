## takeoff
- rotate faster; set 50% and wait for thrust to reach before going flex; cleared for takeoff when lining up — cut the corners, don't make a tight turn *(S1 SIN-DPS 2026-10-27)*

## climb
- Pehon called altitude — don't fly the FDs, especially ALT* or rolling out on heading, because it lags *(S1 SIN-DPS 2026-10-27)*

## cruise
- ATC cannot enforce speed control during a hold; when exiting the hold go back to your ATC speed *(S1 SIN-DPS 2026-10-27)*
- weather deviation — 1-in-60 rule; use your heading to determine the offset: 10 degrees = 10 miles *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- avoiding a super cell — 8000 ft and 60 NM *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- EDTO — fuel leak, smoke, dual engine failure, fire = fixed speed strategy VMO/MMO; other cases, even medical, follow the standard strategy — no rush, more stable approach, no need to overstress the aircraft with a chance of overspeed *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- won't get winds in ARI-ASIA for satcom, usually only SITA Pacific gets the winds; refer FCOM DSC info system — datalink — architecture, scroll down to VHF *(S5 SIN-HAK 2026-11-05)*

## descent
- when reducing speed let the nose go down before reducing speed, get the descent started *(S1 SIN-DPS 2026-10-27)*
- into Singapore was good; can be more precise following time with 500 fpm *(S1 SIN-DPS 2026-10-27)*
- TAS increases about 2-3 kts per 1000 ft of elevation; take Vapp + wind factor, GS/2 gives your V/S, that is the estimated ROD *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- reduce to 250 kts is enough; wait till 25 NM to 3200 *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*

## approach
- direct to the point before the FAF to have a proper turn *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- can configure gear down a bit later, otherwise you are dragging in *(S2 SIN-CJB 2026-10-29)*
- HKT RNAV STAR — 90 degree interception is ok *(S4 SIN-SRG 2026-11-03)*
- hard-tune ANY in case of radial interception *(S4 SIN-SRG 2026-11-03)*

## parking
