# Cards

Obsidian Spaced Repetition single-line format: `Question::Answer`. One card per
line, grouped by phase. Phrase every question as a cockpit situation, not a trivia
question. `route.py` adds C items under **Drafts**; write the answer, then move the
line up into its phase. `cards_export.py` writes `anki.tsv`; `cards_review.py`
schedules them (SM-2, state in `state.json`).
